What changed

Rapier rigid bodies for pelvis, chest, head, upper/lower limbs, hands and feet. Spherical joint constraints connect segments. Neural outputs drive bounded torques on the physical segments, not animation rotations. Ground friction and contact response come from Rapier. No artificial forward gait, height correction, or manual horizontal translation.

Prototype limitations: spherical joints have no anatomical angle limits yet, and motor torques are simple rather than muscle-like. Physics results and learning have not been browser-verified.
